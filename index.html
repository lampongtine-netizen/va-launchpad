02 / EXPLORE YOUR TRACK

to be a VA.

Explore the different directions you can take. You don't have to choose today.

01

General Virtual Assistant

Administrative support, organisation, research, data entry, inboxes and everyday business tasks.

02

Executive Assistant

Higher-level support including calendars, inbox management, coordination, documentation and executive organisation.

Systems, task coordination, documentation, team follow-up and keeping moving parts organised.

04 / FAQ

Before you register.

Do I need VA experience?

No. The introductory webinar is designed for people who are exploring Virtual Assistance, including complete beginners.

Is the webinar free?

Yes. The introductory webinar is free to attend.

Do I need to choose a VA track now?

No. The purpose of the webinar is to help you understand the available paths before deciding what interests you most.

What happens after the webinar?

VA Launchpad plans to offer free track-specific learning so participants can continue developing foundational skills in areas that interest them.

Does joining guarantee a VA job?

No. VA Launchpad is an educational initiative. Participation does not guarantee employment, client placement, or acceptance into the OPSYRA talent pool.
