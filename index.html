04 / FAQ

Before you register.

Do I need VA experience?

No. The introductory webinar is designed for people who are exploring Virtual Assistance, including complete beginners.

Is the webinar free?

Yes. The introductory webinar is free to attend.

Do I need to choose a VA track when I register?

No. Registration is only for your webinar spot. You’ll explore General VA, Executive Assistant, GoHighLevel / CRM and Social Media Management during the session, then choose your training track afterward.

What happens after the webinar?

You’ll receive a separate Track Selection Form where you can choose your first-choice track and an optional second choice. We’ll then use your selection to share the relevant free deeper training details.

Does joining guarantee a VA job?

No. VA Launchpad is an educational initiative. Participation does not guarantee employment, client placement, or acceptance into the OPSYRA talent pool.
